How to read: chain runs Long-term to Short-term: Y, HY, Q, M, W, D. Green = close above that CPR, amber = inside CPR, red = below.
✓ already above (days above)   ⚡ fresh cross (today / days ago)   Yellow outline = fresh.
Small line under each box = this period's CPR vs the previous period's CPR: ▲▲ Higher Value, ▲ Higher (overlap), ▼▼ Lower Value, ▼ Lower (overlap), ◇ Inside, ◈ Outside.
Base-up = ladder starts from Yearly and builds toward Daily. Mid-up = starts from a middle timeframe while higher ones are still not crossed.
CPR for a period is calculated from the previous completed period: Pivot=(H+L+C)/3, BC=(H+L)/2, TC=2*Pivot-BC. Yearly/Half-Yearly/Quarterly use calendar periods.
L3 Zone scan: uses the Camarilla L3 and CPR of the last completed Week / Month / Quarter / Half-Year / Year (these are the levels now in effect). A stock qualifies when L3 is within your % of the Pivot, L3 lies between BC and TC, and the zone (BC to TC) is above the previous close. Watching = zone above price. Touched / Inside / Crossed = the latest daily candle has reached the zone.
